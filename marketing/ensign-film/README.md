# Ensign Studio: "Raise your flag" (39.6 s, 16:9, 2.39:1 letterbox)

- `ensign-raise-your-flag.mp4`: master, 1080p, AAC 256k, -14 LUFS
- `ensign-raise-your-flag-share.mp4`: smaller copy for WhatsApp and email

Built from six Kling 4.0 live-action shots (harbour, baker, shop, electrician, face, flag), real screens from the Ensign site, Northside and IsiMork, and an ElevenLabs voiceover (voice: Brian McManaman).
`film.html` is the compositor (draw(t)), `render.js` renders it to video, `score.py` writes the original score, sound design and voice mix.
Raw clips and frames are not committed; regenerate from the prompts in the session if needed.

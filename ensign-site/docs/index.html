Process

Three weeks from brief to launch

You sign off at the end of each week, so there are no surprises at the end.

Intake

A 45-minute call about your business, customers, products, and the rules that apply. We check your name isn't taken.

Week 1

Identity

Research report, brand identity, and the script and storyboard for your launch ad.

Week 2

Build

Website, shop and CRM on a working preview link, plus the first cut of your ad.

Week 3

Launch

Apps, final ad, end-to-end tests, hosting and domain. Then training, and we keep it running.
